Later Brain — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

The short version: everything you save in Later Brain stays on your phone. There is no account, no server, no cloud, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not even have permission to use the internet, so it cannot send your data anywhere.

Who we are

Later Brain ("the app") is an Android app made by an independent developer. It lets you save links, places, recipes and ideas, and reminds you of them at a useful moment.

What the app stores

The things you choose to save are stored only in a private database on your phone. For each item this can include:

  • a title, a link and any note you add;
  • a category (for example "Recipe" or "Restaurant") and when you would like to be reminded;
  • a place name, and its map coordinates if the link you shared contained them;
  • when you saved it, and whether you marked it as done, snoozed it or dismissed it.

The app also keeps your settings (such as how many reminders per day and your quiet hours) on your phone.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, upload or share any of your data with us or anyone else.
  • It has no account or sign-in.
  • It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools.
  • It does not request the Android INTERNET permission, so it cannot connect to any server.
  • It does not open or download the links you save. Links only open in your browser when you tap them.
  • Your saved items are excluded from Google cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

Permissions

The app only asks for a permission when you use the feature that needs it, and explains why first.

  • Notifications — to show your reminders.
  • Calendar (read-only, optional) — only if you turn on "Use my calendar". The app looks at upcoming events on your phone to spot free time and events that match something you saved. Calendar details are read on the phone, used at that moment, and never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Location (optional, in a future update) — only if you save a place and turn on place reminders. Android tells the app when you arrive near a place you saved. The app does not keep a history of where you have been.
  • Run at start-up — so your reminders keep working after your phone restarts.

Sharing into the app

When you share something to Later Brain from another app (for example Chrome or Google Maps), the app receives only the text or link that you chose to share.

Deleting your data

You can delete any item inside the app. To delete everything, uninstall the app or clear its storage in your phone's Settings. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.
