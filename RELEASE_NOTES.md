# Release Notes

What's new in each version of Later Brain, in plain language.

---

## Version 1.0.0 (in progress): Phase 1, saving things

The first building block: you can save things and see them in one calm list.

**New**
- **Save from any app.** Tap Share in Chrome, Google Maps, YouTube or anywhere else, then pick *Save to Later Brain*. The title and link are filled in for you.
- **Quick add.** Type an idea or a note straight into the app.
- **"When should I remind you?"** Choose *Tonight*, *Weekend*, *When I'm nearby*, *Next time I'm free* or *Surprise me*. (The reminders themselves arrive in the next update.)
- **Automatic category.** The app guesses whether something is a restaurant, recipe, article, video, book, place, trip, shopping or an idea. You can change it with one tap.
- **Google Maps places.** When you share a place from Maps, its name is kept, and so is its exact location when the link includes it.
- **Home screen.** A highlighted *Maybe today* pick, then everything that's *Waiting for the right moment*, then a folded-away *Done* list.
- **Item details.** Open the link, mark it done, bring it back, edit it or delete it.
- **Light and dark mode.** Follows your phone's setting.

**Privacy**
- The app has no internet permission at all, so your saved items physically can't leave your phone.
- Saved items are left out of Google cloud backup.

**Behind the scenes**
- Privacy policy page published at https://ankitnitk.github.io/LaterBrain/privacy-policy.html
- Play Store listing kit added: descriptions, store icon, feature graphic and answers for every Play Console form.
- Built for Android 16 (API level 36), as Google Play now requires for new apps.
